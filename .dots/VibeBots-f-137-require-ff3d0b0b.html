F-137 Require a real exact-head bot review before merge

A merge gate must require one submitted CodeRabbit review on the exact current head, zero unresolved review threads, and the full 60-second settle window. A SUCCESS context, rate-limited summary, or instant command reply without a review node cannot satisfy the gate. Poll GraphQL reviews and threads, verify the summary marker and reviewed commit range, compute retry time through rate limits, require explicit user approval plus durable evidence for any bypass, and cover rate-limited success, stale prior-head review, real no-findings review, actionable thread, resolved finding followed by approval, and manual bypass.
