F-140 Require approval before branch history rewrites

Prevent automation from force-pushing or otherwise rewriting a shared PR branch without explicit user approval. When approved, use force-with-lease, record old and new heads plus the reason, verify no intended commits were lost, and require fresh exact-head CI and review. First evidence: PR #196 force-updated twice at 17:17:14 and 17:20:25 UTC after Quality failed.
