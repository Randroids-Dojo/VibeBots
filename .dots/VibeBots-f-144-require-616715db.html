F-144 Require explicit approval before remote branch deletion

Before deleting a remote branch directly or as a merge side effect, require explicit current approval naming the branch and exact head. Detect repository auto-delete behavior in the merge preflight, prove the head is retained by the intended PR and main history, record the approval and deletion event, verify the ref is absent, and cover direct delete, merge-triggered delete, stale head, already-absent ref, missing approval, and approved cleanup.
