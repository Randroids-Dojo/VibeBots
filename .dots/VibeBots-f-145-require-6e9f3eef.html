F-145 Require referenced followups to exist before merge

For every new F-ID, anchor, and Dot reference introduced by a PR, require the authoritative followup and matching HTML Dot to exist on the target base or the same exact head. If ownership lives on another open PR, expose that dependency and block merge until the base contains it or the authoritative records are coordinated without duplication. Recompute after base or head movement and cover same-PR records, base records, dangling references, missing Dots, cross-PR ordering, and superseded dependencies.
