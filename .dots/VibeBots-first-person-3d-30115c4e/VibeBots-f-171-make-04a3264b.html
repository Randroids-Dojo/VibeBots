F-171 Make bunker part and inventory writes atomic

Replace saveBunkerAndInventory's successful bunker revision write plus later inventory snapshot upserts with one atomic database operation. A winning placement or removal must commit the revision, parts, and exact inventory delta together, or commit none of them. Preserve F-122 conflict behavior: a stale loser changes neither bunker nor inventory and receives the authoritative bunker and inventory. Avoid blind absolute-count upserts that can overwrite a concurrent purchase. Cover injected failure after guard evaluation, concurrent purchase plus placement or removal, a stale revision loser, and successful place, remove, and move paths with authoritative reload assertions.
