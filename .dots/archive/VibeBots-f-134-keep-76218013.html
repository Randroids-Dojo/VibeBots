F-134 Keep the elevator car supporting blocked exits

A boarded lateral action must not drop the miner down the empty rail before the miner actually leaves the car. Preserve car support or reject the exit until the adjacent cell is traversable, keep elevator phase synchronized, and finalize one accepted exit turn exactly once. Cover an open exit, a solid multi-hit wall, a one-hit dig, blocked metal, rock, gas, magma, and boulder cells, both directions, hazard finalization, no fall or collapse while still boarded, and normal gravity only after the miner leaves rail.
