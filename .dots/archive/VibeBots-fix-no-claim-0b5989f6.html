Fix no-claim-on-file when embedded in a cross-site iframe

VibeCoded.games loads vibe-bots.vercel.app in a cross-site iframe. The vb_player cookie was SameSite=Lax, so it was dropped on every request from the iframe and each call minted a fresh player; cash-out then hit 'no claim on file'. Switch the cookie to SameSite=None; Secure; Partitioned (CHIPS).
