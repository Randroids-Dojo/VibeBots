Followups

Backlog spillover discovered during implementation. Keep items PR-sized when possible.

Critical convention. Every followup must carry a data-priority attribute. Three buckets:
  • blocks-release: cannot ship v1 without this.
  • nice-to-have: improves the product but does not block.
  • polish: post-release cleanup.

How to add a followup

<section data-f="F-NNN" data-priority="blocks-release|nice-to-have|polish">
  <h3>F-NNN: Short title</h3>
  <dl>
    <dt>Context</dt><dd>one or two sentences on why this came up.</dd>
    <dt>Blocker (if any)</dt><dd>the condition that prevents working on this now.</dd>
    <dt>Unblock condition</dt><dd>what has to be true to start.</dd>
    <dt>PR / Dot reference</dt><dd>#N or dots-N (when picked up)</dd>
  </dl>
</section>

Keep F-NNN IDs monotonically increasing. When a followup ships, leave the entry in place and append a <dt>Resolved</dt><dd>PR #N</dd> entry. Never delete.

F-007: Combat damage tuning: fights must visibly destroy parts

Context
Fun-factor audit 2026-06-10 (VibeReview session 20260610-220517-4C845606): a full 60s production exhibition match ends with both bots at 100% hull and 4/4 parts. The shove force spreads across wheel contacts that sit below the 35 N threshold, so grind damage is a few points per minute against a 510-point pool. The pitch is "watch those bots fight"; damageless fights fail the core fantasy.
Blocker (if any)
none.
Unblock condition
ready now: re-measure per-contact forces over a full match, retune threshold/scale, and give weapon-category parts a damage multiplier so the spike matters. Acceptance: a default exhibition match destroys at least one part.
PR / Dot reference
combat-legibility slice (PR pending): steering + ram cycles + weapon multiplier + cross-only damage landed; a stock match now grinds the Brawler core below 50% with a clear winner. Part destruction within every stock match is NOT yet guaranteed (the per-event cap that prevents one-shot kills also slows kills); stays open for the active-weapons / next tuning pass.

F-006: Oriented part attachment (90-degree connector steps)

Context
Assembly currently attaches every part with identity rotation at connector offsets (src/sim/assembly.ts). The GDD build loop expects parts to "have orientation"; axis-aligned 90-degree steps keep quaternions exact (components from 0, 0.5, sqrt(0.5), 1) so determinism holds without trig.
Blocker (if any)
none; the starter catalog does not need it yet.
Unblock condition
first part whose silhouette needs rotation (side-mounted spikes, vertical wheels) or the workshop viewport's rotate control.
PR / Dot reference
varied-silhouettes slice
Resolved
Yaw quarter-turns landed on rigid connections (exact quaternions through layout, assembly joints, and the workshop Rotate control); axle connections stay unoriented by validity rule. Pitch/roll steps can follow the same pattern when a part needs them. (varied-silhouettes slice, PR pending)
