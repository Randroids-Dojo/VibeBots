Followups

Backlog spillover discovered during implementation. Keep items PR-sized when possible.

Critical convention. Every followup must carry a data-priority attribute. Three buckets:
  • blocks-release: cannot ship v1 without this.
  • nice-to-have: improves the product but does not block.
  • polish: post-release cleanup.

How to add a followup

<section data-f="F-NNN" data-priority="blocks-release|nice-to-have|polish">
  <h3>F-NNN: Short title</h3>
  <dl>
    <dt>Context</dt><dd>one or two sentences on why this came up.</dd>
    <dt>Blocker (if any)</dt><dd>the condition that prevents working on this now.</dd>
    <dt>Unblock condition</dt><dd>what has to be true to start.</dd>
    <dt>PR / Dot reference</dt><dd>#N or dots-N (when picked up)</dd>
  </dl>
</section>

Keep F-NNN IDs monotonically increasing. When a followup ships, leave the entry in place and append a <dt>Resolved</dt><dd>PR #N</dd> entry. Never delete.

F-020: Walking left or right teleported the miner to the surface row, then it eased back down the shaft

Context
User-reported 2026-06-11: any lateral step snapped the rendered miner to the top of the screen and animated it quickly back down. Root cause in src/components/mine-canvas.tsx: the miner group carried a JSX position prop with Y hardcoded to 0 while useFrame eased the same transform imperatively; column changes made R3F re-apply the prop, resetting Y to the surface mid-glide. Vertical digs never changed the prop's X, which is why only lateral moves showed it.
Blocker (if any)
none.
Unblock condition
resolved.
PR / Dot reference
dot VibeBots-mine-render-fix-e2addc40
Resolved
useFrame is now the sole writer of the miner transform (snap on first frame, ease after); the canvas exposes data-miner-x/y and the e2e suite walks left at depth 2 sampling every frame, asserting the glide never lifts toward the surface. The test was verified to fail against the buggy code (observed Y rose to -0.77 from depth 2) and pass against the fix. PR #26.
