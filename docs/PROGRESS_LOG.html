Combat Legibility (fun-factor audit response)

Branch
feature/combat-legibility
PR
pending
Changed
response to the first VibeReview playtest (session docs/reviews/20260610-220517-4C845606), which found 60s exhibition matches ending with both bots at 100% hull. Root causes found by force probes: wheels (radius 0.4) protruded past the core face and ate every frontal contact below threshold; bots could not steer, so after the first clash they drifted onto parallel tracks and never re-engaged; the first impact burst was the only damage source (one-shot or nothing). Landed: weapon-first differential-drive steering (quaternion-rotated facing, cross-product steer, behind-hemisphere turns, all pure arithmetic), ram cycles (charge, back off, re-charge), damage restricted to bot-versus-bot contacts, weapon damage multiplier (x6), per-event damage cap (45) so the opening clash cannot one-shot, retuned threshold (20 N) and scale (0.22) from measured force distributions, wheel radius 0.34, core durability 180, SIM_VERSION 2. Second stock design (Brawler) because identical mirror bots always draw; the exhibition is now Brawler vs Rammer with design-name HUD labels, per-part damage pips, and timeout banners showing the deciding totals (epsilon-close totals are honest draws).
Verification
dash check, purity check, pnpm typecheck, pnpm lint, pnpm test (34; new stock-matchup test asserts the fight is destructive: Brawler loses over 20% of total durability and the Rammer wins), pnpm build, pnpm test:e2e. Tuning probes ran full 3600-tick matches against measured force distributions.
Assumptions
ground and self contacts deal no damage until arena hazards exist; per-event cap 45 trades guaranteed part destruction for multi-clash fights (F-007 stays open); steering gain 2, backoff 60 ticks at 0.8 speed.
GDD coverage
build-log entries on 02 (Brawler design, wheel sizing) and 03 (steering, ram cycles, damage model); REQ-001 strengthened in place (still partial).
Followups
F-008 and F-009 resolved; F-007 remains open with a progress note (part destruction not yet guaranteed per match).

Match End and Scoring

Branch

PR
pending
Changed
matches now resolve (REQ-005): a 60-second default time limit (configurable per match) with score-based judgment on timeout; end status carries the reason (disable or timeout) plus both score cards.
