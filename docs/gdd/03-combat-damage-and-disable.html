Combat Damage and Disable

Status: partial

Spec

Combat is fully autonomous after match start (REQ-001): no steering, aiming, or firing input exists anywhere in the combat surface. The player's influence ends at the design and its behavior parameters. Damage is physical (REQ-004): parts have durability, take damage from real contact forces in the deterministic sim, and destruction has mechanical consequences.

Damage model

  • Each part instance enters combat with health equal to its catalog durability.
  • Contact forces above a threshold deal damage proportional to the excess force to both parts in the contact. Rolling and resting contacts stay below the threshold.
  • Damage can also be applied directly (future actuated weapons use the same entry point).

Destruction and detachment

  • A part at zero health is destroyed. Non-core parts physically detach: the joint to their parent is removed and the part (with anything mounted on it) becomes loose debris in the arena.
  • Deaths are processed in design part order each tick, keeping replays deterministic.

Disable conditions

  • Core destroyed: the bot is disabled immediately.
  • Immobilized: a bot whose design includes mobility parts is disabled when all of them are destroyed.
  • A disabled bot's motors stop. When either bot is disabled the match is over; both disabled in the same tick is a draw (time-limit scoring is the next slice, REQ-005).

What this does NOT cover yet

  • Time limit and score-based judgment (REQ-005, match-end-and-scoring slice).
  • Steering: the controller stub drives straight toward the opponent; differential drive and behavior parameters come with the behavior slice.
  • Damage degradation short of destruction (e.g. weakened motors), armor, and damage types.
