Test Arena

Status: partial

Spec

Players can test a valid bot against a CPU robot before online combat (REQ-009). The test fight runs the same deterministic match the server will later use for official results: same assembly, same controller, same damage model. The workshop is the entry point: build, validate, test, adjust, repeat.

Debrief

A test fight ends with a debrief: a headline (won, lost, or drew; by knockout or decision; when) and at most two lessons drawn from the match teardown in a fixed order (a weapon that never connected or no weapon at all; the part that went first and what took it; the part that soaked half or more of the damage; a decision lost on the score; for a win, the surviving part that came home weakest). Each lesson has a fix-it action that returns the player to the bench with the relevant part in hand, the weak part selected, the Tune tab open, or a temperament change applied (a decision lost off the front foot offers more aggression; a knockout taken in the pocket offers more patience; both undoable, both opening Tune so the slider is seen), so build, test, learn, tweak is one loop. The lessons are deterministic rules over the same teardown the inspection sheet shows.

The fight ladder

The Test fight menu offers the stock opponents as a ladder, easiest first for the first build the guide hands a player (the Cube Rammer), each rung with a hint of what it asks: Brawler (warm-up), Contagion (controls the floor), Night Terror (all blade), Bulldozer (outshoves a spike), Impaler (punishes a spike), Gravestone (punishes a lance). The win and loss boundary is measured by the deterministic sim and pinned by a test (the first build beats the first three rungs and loses to the last three; a lance in the spike's place beats Impaler and loses to Gravestone; a tempered lance with a ballast tail beats Gravestone), so a sim change that moves the boundary is caught; the order among the rungs on each side of it is by what each bot asks of a build, not by a pinned margin. A rival (another player's saved bot) sits after the ladder.

Arenas

A fight runs in an arena: the shared floor plus a ring of bounce walls whose half extent, height, and restitution are the arena's spec. The Ring is the original floor (walls at nine metres, restitution 0.55) and every fight before arenas existed ran in it; the Pit is a tight floor with hard walls (six metres, 0.8) where nothing has room to flank and every shove rebounds. A ladder rung may name its arena (Contagion fights in the Pit; the rest fight in the Ring), the picker says so on the rung, the stage boots that arena and draws its deck and walls from the same spec the colliders use, the resolve route verifies the fight in the same arena, and the match record stores it. The Ring is byte for byte the arena it was, so the sim version and every stored result hold. A rung moves arena only when the measured claims the ladder makes about it (the starter build's result, its counters) hold in the new arena.

What this does NOT cover yet

  • Match seeds or variations (every test replays the same deterministic fight for a given design and arena).
  • Choosing an arena for a rival fight or an exhibition (those run in the Ring); arena hazards or floor features beyond the walls.
  • Carrying results anywhere (rewards and persistence are later slices).
