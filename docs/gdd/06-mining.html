Mining

Status: partial

Spec

Mining is the primary resource path (REQ-006): a deterministic 2D vertical grid rendered as low-poly 3D blocks from a side camera (Q-004 resolution). The player descends, digs, and decides when to turn back. The design below is the 2026-06-11 research pass (see docs/research/mining-mechanics.html and the baseline VibeReview findings F-012 to F-017): the goal is the "one more trip" engine of the genre's best mobile entries, kept fully deterministic so the server can replay any trip from (seed, gear, action log).

The trip loop (core, unchanged)

  • Every dig and move costs robot battery charge. Banking happens only on the surface, which also recharges the miner.
  • Carried loot is lost on collapse (battery drained underground); banked loot is safe. The trip-back decision is the core tension.
  • Moving up requires a cleared shaft AND ladders (REQ-020, user-directed change 2026-06-11; supersedes the implicit-ladder rule): climbing out of a cell consumes and plants a ladder there once, after which that cell climbs free for the rest of the trip. There is still no digging upward.
  • Light: rows within lantern radius of the miner are fully readable; zoom-out can render a short falloff band beyond that radius where real generated cells sit under a dark gradient. Stronger Lantern upgrades increase both the fully lit range and the useful overview, while far outer rows stay effectively black until approached.
  • Same seed, same mine, same finds: a trip is a pure function of (seed, gear, action log), and the server replays it to credit cash-outs.

Ore tiers and strata (REQ-011, REQ-012)

  • Seven ore tiers with roughly exponential credit value and rarity inverse to value: coal 1, copper 3, silver 8, emerald 20, ruby 50, diamond 125, core crystal 320. Each tier lives in a depth band with overlap, so the previous tier stays present one band deeper and the next tier teases at a band's bottom edge.
  • Named strata roughly every 12 rows (Topsoil, Clay Beds, Old Granite, Glow Caverns, Magma Verge, ...), each with its own dirt palette so descent is visible without reading the HUD. Reaching a stratum for the first time in a trip shows a banner; the first time ever, it pays a one-time milestone bonus credited at banking (deterministic from max depth reached).
  • The wallet currency is "Vibe-Brainiums" ("vibes" for short, user-directed 2026-06-15); ores convert to vibes when banked (Q-005 resolved). The DB column stays players.emeralds as an implementation detail. Banked part caches still grant robot parts (REQ-007).
  • A persistent deepest-depth record lives on the player profile.

Hardness and pickaxe gates (REQ-013)

  • Blocks have a hit count by material (user-directed 2026-06-12: dirt 4, ore 5, cache 6, rock 5 at pickaxe level 1; each pickaxe level removes one swing, min 1). Each swing is one logged action; per-swing energy keeps the block total at the old one-swing cost, so trip economy is unchanged and better pickaxes also save energy. Partial damage renders as crack states.
  • Rock comes in tiers keyed to pickaxe tiers, Terraria style: below the required pickaxe tier the block does not break at all ("granite, my pick can't cut it"); at tier, it digs in a few hits. The player should hit a visible wall one band before they can afford the answer.

Gear upgrades (REQ-013, REQ-014)

  • Persistent per-player tracks bought with credits at the surface shop, superlinear costs (roughly x3 per tier): pickaxe (gates rock tiers, fewer hits), battery cell (max robot charge), cargo hold (carry capacity), lantern (light radius), plus specialized tracks such as Warpcoil, Blast Charge, Elevator Speed, and Fall Harness.
  • Carry capacity is the secondary trip cap: a full hold no longer blocks digging, but any ore that does not fit falls to the nearest surface as a floor pile for later pickup, so banking is still the only way to convert the carry safely.
  • Gear levels are part of the sim input. A trip is replayed with the gear snapshot taken at session start; the server validates the snapshot against owned gear at cash-out (Q-007). Buying a surface upgrade after a mined trip automatically banks that trip first, then applies the upgraded gear to the fresh trip immediately.

Hazards (REQ-015)

  • Falling rock (user-directed 2026-06-14: "rocks should fall a few seconds after the dirt beneath them is mined away"; tuned 2026-06-16 to "after two moves instead of one"): any rock or boulder cell whose support is dug out teeters in place, then drops after two player moves, either crushing the miner it lands on (ends the trip like a collapse, carry lost, hauled up) or coming to rest as a path-blocking obstacle. The delay is counted in player actions, not wall-clock time, so the trip stays a pure function of (seed, gear, actions) and the server replay agrees; the escalating tremble is the tell, the hazard-free top rows never drop, and a fallen rock keeps its pickaxe-tier gate where it lands. Falls resolve as discrete grid steps from the action log: no physics engine and no reflex demand, since standing still is always safe.
  • Miner free fall (user-directed 2026-06-17): when support disappears or the miner walks into an unsupported empty shaft, the miner falls until landing on a filled cell, ladder, or plank. A base miner survives falls up to 4 cells; a fall of more than 4 cells ends the trip like a falling-rock crush, with carry lost and death-recovery ladder/plank stock applied. The Fall Harness upgrade raises the safe fall distance while staying part of the replayable gear snapshot.
  • Gas pockets: digging adjacent to a gas cell vents it; it detonates in a plus shape, destroying blocks (and their loot) and draining robot battery charge. Gas can chain into gas. Reads as a slightly-off block so depth knowledge becomes player skill.
  • Hazard frequency grows with depth; no reflex-based hazards (one-thumb friendly).

Consumables (REQ-016)

  • Bought at the surface shop, carried into trips, used as logged actions: dynamite (clears a diamond through rock at any pickaxe tier and SCOOPS the ore and part-caches it breaks into the hold; ore past the hold's capacity stays piled on the floor to walk over later) and recall rope (ends the trip from anywhere, keeping carry, banking normally). The blast starts as the classic 5-cell plus and widens by one ring per level of the Blast Charge gear track. Priced at roughly 10-30% of an average trip haul.
  • Each resolves one dread: "I can't get through" (dynamite), "I won't make it back" (recall). Insurance converts anxiety into economy without deleting tension (F-015's fix).
  • Abandon (REQ-025, user-directed 2026-06-12): the free escape valve under both. Below ground, no consumable, two-tap confirm: the crew hauls you up and the carry stays behind, exactly a collapse but chosen. Being out of ladders and rope is a loss of cargo, never a softlock of time.

Ladders and planks: traversal aids (REQ-020, REQ-022)

  • Ladders are bought at the shop (2 cr); trips no longer ship a free bundle (user-directed 2026-06-15). A brand-new player is gifted one starting bundle (8 ladders, 4 planks) at account creation so the first descent works, but it is a one-time gift, never re-granted on selling. After that the one free source is dying in the mine (battery out or crushed, not giving up): a death tops the ladder stock back up TO 8 so the miner can climb out. Free death-rungs do not bank between trips; only purchased ladders carry over.
  • Planted ladders persist in the cell and render in the shaft; standing on one lets the player pick it back up as a logged action, and collect mode lets the player tap or click visible placed ladder cells, mark them in-world, then confirm one logged pickup action. A falling boulder smashes any ladder in the cell it lands in. Ladder placement is blocked while riding the elevator rail.
  • Out of ladders below ground, the options are honest: buy more next trip, spend a recall rope now, give up the carry with Abandon, or run the robot battery dry and lose the carry (and get refilled to 8 on the death). The HUD prices the climb home in charge AND ladders, warning when either is short.
  • Server replay parity: ladder spend is part of the deterministic action log; cash-out decrements the purchased portion (used minus what deaths granted this trip, floored at zero), so death-refilled rungs are never charged.
  • Planks (REQ-022, user-directed 2026-06-16; supersedes the auto-place rule from 2026-06-11): planks deploy only from the explicit plank button, in the miner's facing direction. The button is disabled without plank stock, while riding the elevator rail, or when the facing cell and its below cell are both non-empty. If the facing cell is solid but the cell below is empty, the player can pre-place the plank on that solid cell; when that block is mined, the plank remains and the miner walks onto it without falling. Without a support, lateral movement can still enter the cell and then falls through empty cells until supported, or dies if the fall exceeds the current Fall Harness limit. Planks can be collected back into inventory from visible range by tapping or clicking their visible cells in collect mode, then confirming the selected pickup. Planks are bought at the shop (2 cr) with no per-trip freebie; a death refills plank stock up to 4 the same way ladders refill to 8. Boulders smash planks like ladders; the surface walk row is boardwalked (village walking never needs planks). Same death-grant carryover and replay-parity rules as ladders. Ladders count as support (fixed 2026-06-12, user-reported): a ladder in the target cell is held onto and one in the cell below tops out underfoot, so no plank is needed over a laddered shaft.

Touch-first controls (REQ-023)

  • One-thumb play: pressing anywhere on the mine view spawns a virtual thumbstick under the finger (VibeKit's virtual-joystick module); pushing past the deadzone fires the dominant cardinal direction immediately, then repeats while held. Release dismisses it. Buttons, stall menus, and panels stack above the touch surface and keep their normal taps. Repeated taps, quick key releases, held keys, and held thumbstick movement all pass through the same directional cadence gate, with Pickaxe upgrades making walking and mining slightly faster without allowing tap-spam bypasses.
  • Camera zoom is a render preference, not a sim input: scroll wheel, two-finger pinch, and trigger or shoulder plus D-pad gamepad input adjust it. Zoomed cells use the real deterministic mine generation and sparse diff, so a distant dark-edge cell is the same cell the miner reaches later.
  • The thumbstick routes through the same action path as the keyboard and buttons, so a primed dynamite throws toward the flicked direction.
  • Game text never selects: long-presses and drags must not open the platform copy/share callout. Editable fields (design names) opt back into selection.
  • Control copy is pointer-aware (user-directed 2026-06-12): coarse-pointer devices never see keyboard mentions; the onboarding hint reads "drag anywhere to move" on touch and adds the WASD note only on fine pointers.
  • HUD discipline (REQ-024): glanceable chips, not paragraphs. Depth and stratum, a battery-charge bar, hold count, carry value, and the climb cost (charge + ladders, red when short) live in thin chips; action feedback is a single one-line toast; consumables are icon buttons in thumb reach; there is no page header, no D-pad, and no standing help text (a one-shot "drag to move" hint disappears after the first action). Banked haul auto-sells when the miner reaches the surface, so the HUD shows one spendable vibe wallet instead of a separate banked-vibes counter. Sim numbers ride data attributes so tests never pin chip copy.
  • HUD vibes and climb readout (user-directed 2026-06-16): the spendable vibe wallet is always shown as the leading chip (it used to appear only inside an open stall sheet), and the cryptic combined climb-cost chip (up-arrow, charge bolt, ladder) was removed; the battery bar and the ladder count still carry that information.

The surface village (REQ-021)

  • The surface row is a place, not a menu bar: the miner walks between shop stalls drawn in the scene (Buyer at the left, the shaft headframe center, Supply Depot and Upgrades to the right). Standing on a stall's column opens its menu; walking away closes it.
  • Buyer: explains the surface banking rule. Haul sells automatically as soon as the miner reaches the surface and the vibes go straight to the wallet; the stall no longer requires a manual sell step.
  • Supply Depot: dynamite, rope, and ladders at REQ-016/REQ-020 prices, packed straight into the current trip (Q-008 rule B). The sheet separates "have" counts from selected buy quantity, with quick buys for 1, 5, or 10 items at a time.
  • Upgrades: the permanent gear tracks; if the current surface trip has mined actions, the purchase banks that trip first, then applies the upgraded gear immediately to the fresh trip.
  • Without storage the stalls stay browsable with an honest "ledger offline" line; nothing is charged.
  • Shop feel (user-directed 2026-06-12): stall menus are mobile bottom sheets that slide up with thumb-sized rows (icon tile, name, blurb, owned or level badge, big price button) and the wallet in the header; buildings sit back in depth with a lit doorstep so the miner stands at the door instead of clipping the facade, and the door lights while the shop is open.
  • Destination buildings (user-directed 2026-06-16): the surface village is the overworld hub. Two buildings stand at the edges of the row, a Workshop on the far left and a Battles arena on the far right; standing on one shows a floating "Enter" button that routes to that screen instead of opening a stall sheet. Every entered screen returns with a "Back to mine" button, the top nav bar is gone from every page, and the landing page redirects to the mine.
  • Buying robot parts moved into the Workshop (user-directed 2026-06-16): the standalone Part Shop page is gone. Gear is bought at the Upgrades stall and supplies at the Supply Depot, so the in-Workshop shop is parts only; vibes come from reaching the surface with mine haul.
  • Offscreen base return (user-directed 2026-06-17): when the miner is on the surface and no village building is visible, an animated edge indicator points back toward the village. Tapping it opens a Base return menu with a small distance-scaled vibe cost, disables purchase when the wallet is short or offline, confirms before spending, then drops the miner at the shaft center with teleport SFX and a burst effect.

Trip-decision legibility (REQ-017)

  • The HUD shows carried value in credits (the bet on the table) and an estimated return cost in battery charge for the climb home; the battery readout warns when charge approaches the return cost (F-012's fix).
  • The collapse rules are stated in-world before the first descent (one line on the surface), not discovered by losing.

Dig feel and fanfare (REQ-018, REQ-019)

  • Render-layer juice, never sim: crack states on multi-hit blocks, debris particles and shake scaled to hardness, tweened miner movement (no teleporting), action-specific SFX, pitch-ramped pickup chimes, slow-mo sting and banner on part-cache finds.
  • Collapse is a drama beat (battery warning, dim, "the crew hauls you up"), and every failed trip ends with a near-miss reveal: what you were carrying and the treasure that was almost in reach (the client has the full grid; pure UI).

The persistent endless mine (REQ-026, REQ-027)

  • The mine never resets (user-directed 2026-06-12: "it doesn't feel rewarding to see the mine reset every time I sell"). Auto-selling at the surface banks credits and parts but the carved world stays: every tunnel, ladder, plank, and structure persists forever. The trail IS the trophy (SteamWorld Dig 2 lesson).
  • The world is endless both horizontally and vertically: generation stays a pure function of (seed, col, row) over all integers, the village anchors at the origin columns, and the camera follows wherever the trail goes.
  • Persistence model (Q-009 default): the world state is a sparse diff over pure generation (dug cells plus placed structures); the server stores the diff per player and replays each trip's action log on top of it to credit cash-outs, so determinism and anti-cheat survive without replaying a lifetime log. Guests persist the diff locally and adopt it server-side on first cash-out.
  • A trip is now surface-to-surface; consumables are bought and carry over per descent, not per world. The free ladder/plank refill is a death-recovery, not a per-trip grant (user-directed 2026-06-15).

The transport ladder (REQ-028 elevator, REQ-029 teleporter)

  • Progression arc: ladders and planks carry the early game; the elevator compresses the mid-game commute; range-limited warp beacons compress the late game; each tier is a visible investment in the world.
  • Elevator (REQ-028): one per player, an elevator tower on its own surface column beside the village. Depth is bought in segments at the tower (superlinear pricing per stratum reached); building it carves and rails its column. If a rail segment passes through existing laddered or planked cells in that column, those supports are removed from the world and returned to inventory. A one-time account cleanup scans already owned rail spans and returns any ladder or plank that was previously hidden behind the elevator. Riding is free, logged, and automatic: starting a ride sends the miner to the rail bottom or surface without repeated button presses. Each logged ride step still travels a fixed number of rows so the descent is visible, but the UI chains those steps until the endpoint. The base car covers 6 rows per step, and Elevator Speed increases both rows per step and automatic step cadence so the path still scales to a 1000-row rail. Ride-down stops at the rail bottom; ride-up banks the carry when the car lands topside. The shaft is part of the persistent world.
  • Teleporter (REQ-029): late game. Beacon kits are consumables (priced like big dynamite); placing one plants a beacon at the miner's cell (one active beacon; replanting moves it). The village gains a warp pad; warping between pad and beacon is free and logged but only works while the beacon's depth is within the warpcoil's range. The warpcoil is a persistent gear track upgraded over time (range tiers roughly 60, 150, 400, 1000 rows).
  • Transport never skips unconquered space: elevators only descend rail you bought, beacons only plant where you stand. Range and segment pricing keep depth earned.

Deeper dangers and richer rewards (REQ-030)

  • Strata continue past the Magma Verge (Ashfall Galleries, the Black Seam, Echo Vaults, Core Approach, ...), each with palettes, first-reach bonuses, and a new pressure: magma pockets (vent like gas but burn triple), rock tier 4+ gating pickaxe 5+, denser hazard rolls.
  • Part caches scale with depth: deeper bands roll from richer part tables, so the bot-building reward track (REQ-007) keeps paying the deeper you push.

The no-softlock floor (REQ-031)

  • A broke miner is never stuck: a new player is gifted a starting ladder/plank bundle (8/4) once, Abandon is always available below ground (free, forfeits only the carry), and dying refills the ladder/plank stock up to 8/4 for free, so a player who runs dry can always surface and recover (user-directed 2026-06-15 moved the recurring free stock from a per-trip grant to a death-recovery, keeping only the one-time starting gift). Being out of money is a setback measured in bought ladders or one dead-battery refill, never a dead account.
  • Legibility: the Supply Depot states that ladders and planks cost vibes and that dying refills them, and a zero-ladders surface state surfaces a toast pointing at the depot and the death-refill.

What this does NOT cover yet

  • Smelt combos (consecutive matching ores multiply value), surface accrual between trips, collections/museum screen, disguised hazard blocks beyond gas, server-issued seeds (F-011).
  • Music and authored audio assets stay outside scope for now; mining action SFX are synthesized in the client.
