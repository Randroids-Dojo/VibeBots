Mining

Status: partial

Spec

Mining is the primary resource path (REQ-006): a deterministic 2D vertical grid rendered as low-poly 3D blocks from a side camera (Q-004 resolution). The player descends, digs, and decides when to turn back.

The trip loop

  • Every dig and move costs lamp energy. Dirt digs; rock is undiggable at pickaxe level 1 (route around it; upgrades later).
  • Emeralds and rare part caches (REQ-007) ride in the carry until banked. Banking happens only on the surface, which also refills the lamp.
  • Running dry underground is a collapse: the carry is lost, the crew hauls you up. The trip-back decision is the core tension.
  • Moving up requires a cleared shaft (ladders are implicit in cells you dug).
  • Light: only rows within lantern radius of the miner render; deeper rows are dark until approached.
  • Depth scaling: rock, emeralds, and caches all grow more common with depth.

What this does NOT cover yet

  • Pickaxe and lantern upgrades, placeable ladders, hazards.
  • Selling emeralds or using found parts (economy slice; banked loot is display-only).
  • Persistence (a refresh forgets the mine).
